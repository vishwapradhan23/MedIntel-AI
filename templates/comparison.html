Instance-Based Learning (KNN)

K-Nearest Neighbors ($k=7$) operates on non-parametric instance memory. When classifying a patient query vector $\mathbf{x}$, it computes the Euclidean distance: $$d(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{x}_i) = \sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^{d} (x_j - x_{i,j})^2}$$ Points closer in scaled feature space exert greater inverse-distance weight, providing strong non-linear boundary adaptability.

Bayesian Learning (Naïve Bayes)

Gaussian Naïve Bayes computes posterior disease class probabilities using Bayes' Theorem with conditional independence assumptions: $$P(C_k \mid \mathbf{x}) \propto P(C_k) \prod_{j=1}^{d} \mathcal{N}(x_j; \mu_{kj}, \sigma_{kj}^2)$$ Extremely fast at inference time and highly resilient to over-fitting on small datasets.
